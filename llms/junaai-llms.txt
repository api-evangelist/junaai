# Juna.ai

> Juna.ai is a Berlin-based industrial AI company building an Agentic Factory OS — a platform of autonomous AI agents that plan, steer, and continuously improve complex industrial production processes. Agents ingest live plant data through integrations with existing IT/OT systems (SAP, Siemens, AVEVA, PI Server, Snowflake), model assets into a Factory Graph, detect anomalies, run root-cause analysis, and recommend or execute control actions 24/7 with human-in-the-loop oversight. Juna does not currently publish a public developer API, SDK, or documentation portal; integration is delivered through enterprise engagements. Backed by Kleiner Perkins, Norrsken VC, and John Doerr.

## Product
- [Factory Agents](https://juna.ai/agents): Autonomous AI agents that monitor processes, optimize energy use, and improve quality.
- [Factory Foundation](https://juna.ai/foundation): Unified factory management and data foundation (Factory Graph + Knowledge Graph).
- [Factory Chat](https://juna.ai/chat): Natural-language interface for analyzing factory data.
- [Enterprise](https://juna.ai/enterprise): Enterprise deployment, hosting, and compliance posture.
